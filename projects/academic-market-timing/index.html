METHODOLOGY

A timing instrument, not an acceptance-rate oracle.

01

Hiring supply

Daily discipline-level vacancy counts are compared with each field's own rolling history. This avoids treating a naturally large field as automatically “easy.”

02

Sentiment

Hiring-expansion and hiring-stress language is scored from public news RSS, with an optional community RSS layer when available.

03

Macro backdrop

U.S. private educational-services job-openings data provide a slower-moving macro labor-demand anchor.

04

Timing & forecast

A transparent weighted index combines supply momentum, sentiment and macro conditions. Forward values use bounded recent trend extrapolation and report confidence separately.

Interpretation: “Competition pressure” is a market-tightness proxy, not applicants-per-opening. The dashboard is designed to answer when the market is improving or deteriorating, not to estimate your personal probability of receiving an offer.
