How to play

  1. One player creates a room and shares the 6-character code.
  2. The second player joins from another device.
  3. Each round shows a target number and a shuffled grid.
  4. Click the target before your opponent. A wrong click locks you briefly.
  5. First player to 10 points wins the match.
